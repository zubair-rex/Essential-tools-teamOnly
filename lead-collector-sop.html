Phase 1 — Quick screening
Should I even look at this person?
0/8
Numbers check
Do they have more than 8,000 followers?
Sweet spot is 8k–100k. Mega accounts (500k+) usually already work with agencies — harder to land.
Do their last 5–10 posts average at least 18–20 real comments?
Look at comment quality — fire emojis and "great post!" don't count. Look for: does people finding this content helpful? Comment replies are positive or people asking helps on that topic or actual questions about the topic.
Are they posting at least 2–3 reels per week?
Exception: if engagement is very strong (good comments or high comments like 80-150 comments), don't disqualify someone posting once a week.
Offer check
Do they sell a coaching program, course, or paid service?
If no paid offer exists — stop here. No product means no prospect. Move on immediately.
Are they in a monetizable niche?
Good niches: business, health, fitness, relationships, money, mindset, career. Avoid: travel, pets, art, memes — hobby audiences rarely spend $500+ on programs.
Is their offer clear and specific?
Good: "I help 9-5 employees replace their income in 90 days". Weak: "I help people with mindset". Vague positioning = weak offer. Lower priority.
Is their price point $500 or higher? (if visible)
High-ticket coaches ($1k+) are better prospects than people selling $27 PDFs. They have more revenue to invest in a funnel.
Hard gate: If they fail checks 1, 4, or 5 (no followers, no paid offer, wrong niche) — stop immediately. Do not go to Phase 2. Move to the next prospect.
02
Phase 2 — Content analysis
Are they an authority or just an entertainer?
0/4
How to do this: Watch 6-8 of their most recent reels. Read the captions. Skim the comments. This should take about 3–4 minutes.
Watch their content and check what applies
Do they share client results, transformations, or success stories? (this can be on highlighlits section too, it usually stays on highlights not in contents)
Social proof in content = people are already buying from them. Their audience trusts them. This is a very good sign.
Do they teach valuable content and position themselves as the expert?
Look for: tips, step-by-step frameworks, "how I did X" stories. Authority content sells. Entertainment doesn't.
Are people in the comments asking how to work with them, or asking follow-up questions? or commenting about they got help from this content or they need help because they're facing some problem
Buying-intent comments are the strongest signal you can find. Examples: "Where do I sign up?", "Do you take 1:1 clients?", "How much does your program cost?"
Do they use story-based hooks — a problem they had and how they solved it? (What I mean is, is the content on their niche and helpful or it's just entertaining purpose?)
Creators who already use storytelling respond much better to our pitch. They understand copy. Our funnel will perform better for them too.
Tip: You don't need all four. But if none of A, B, or C are true — they are likely an entertainer, not an authority. Deprioritise them or skip.
03
Phase 3 — Funnel analysis
How are they currently selling? Do they need us?
0/0
Goal of this phase: Find the gap. A big audience with a broken or missing funnel = your ideal prospect. Read every step carefully.
Step 1 — Find their call to action (CTA)

Look at their bio and recent captions. What are they telling people to do? Match what you see to one of the outcomes below.

DM
CTA says "DM me to get a free call" or something like this
They have no funnel at all. Every sale is done manually through direct messages. This is a massive opportunity.
Add prospectNote: "No funnel — selling via DMs only. but has a big offer" This is a strong lead.
BIO
CTA says "link in bio" → open the link
The link will take you to one of three places. Identify which one and follow the matching action below.
Direct sales pageSelling straight from content — no email funnel in between. Add as prospect.
Lead magnet / opt-inThey're collecting emails. Go to Step 2.
General websiteA website is NOT a funnel. Check if there's an opt-in form anywhere on the site. If yes → Step 2. If no → add as prospect.
Only continue if they have a lead magnet or opt-in
Step 2 — Test their email sequence

Before signing up — quickly read their opt-in page. Is the copy compelling? (if you understand good copy then do it) Does it make you want to sign up? Note it down. Then use a test email address and sign up.
